## What Validus is

Validus Investment Holdings Pte Ltd (UEN 201803167H, 12 Marina View #12-02(G),
Asia Square Tower 2, Singapore 018961) owns and operates SME supply-chain
financing businesses across Southeast Asia. Products are invoice financing,
purchase-order financing, vendor/supply-chain financing programs, working
capital loans, SME business accounts and corporate cards, funded by accredited
and institutional investors. The group reports more than US$5 billion funded
across its markets since 2015.

## Corporate status (important for anyone resolving this name)

- Validus Capital Pte Ltd, the SINGAPORE subsidiary and the entity named
  "Validus Capital", was acquired outright by GXS Bank and the deal completed
  on 15 April 2025. It now trades as GXS Capital. https://gxscapital.sg/
- validus.sg and www.validus.sg now 301 to the group site https://validusgrp.com/
- The group (Validus Investment Holdings) continues to operate the other three
  markets and is a distinct entity from GXS Bank.
- Do not confuse this company with Validus Risk Management (validusrm.com, FX
  and rate risk for private capital), Validus Holdings (Bermuda reinsurance),
  or Validus Fintech Services Private Limited (India), which publishes the
  `validus-ui-core` and `validus-central-error-validation` npm packages. None
  of those are this company.

## API surface

There is none that is public. Verified 2026-09-02:

- No developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/Protobuf
  /WSDL contract on validusgrp.com, validus.vn, batumbu.id, siamvalidus.co.th
  or gxscapital.sg. /openapi.json, /swagger.json and /api-docs return 404 on
  every one of them.
- No developer.* or docs.* subdomain resolves on any Validus domain.
- api.batumbu.id resolves and serves a Go backend, but every probed path
  (including /openapi.json, /docs, /graphql, /health) returns a plain-text
  "404 page not found". It is a private application backend with no public
  contract, no documentation and no published base path.
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no /llms.txt published by the company.
- The only machine-readable surface Validus serves is the WordPress REST API
  of its own marketing site, https://validusgrp.com/wp-json/ (HTTP 200, 248
  routes, wp/v2 and plugin namespaces). That is CMS default behaviour, not a
  product API, and API Evangelist does not record it as one.
